Cookie policy

This site uses no cookies. Nor does it use any other technology to store or read information in your browser or on your device: no localStorage, no sessionStorage, no IndexedDB and no tracking pixels.

Why there is no cookie banner

The law (Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE, which implements the EU ePrivacy Directive) requires asking for your consent before storing or reading information on your device when it is not strictly necessary. As this site stores and reads nothing, there is nothing to accept or reject, which is why you see no banner.

Third parties

The site includes no third-party content that could set cookies, such as fonts, icons, videos, analytics or advertising: everything is served from the site itself. On the public site, the hosting provider (Vercel) sets no cookies either.

If this changes

If the site ever needed cookies that are not essential, your consent would be requested before using them and this page would be updated. More information in the privacy policy.
