In short: your CV never leaves your browser. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or storage. The only thing logged is the technical connection data the hosting server needs to deliver the page.

Controller

The data controller is the owner of the CV Generator, who can be reached at %SITE_EMAIL%. More details in the legal notice.

What you write in your CV

Everything you write (name, contact details, experience and the rest of the CV) is processed only in your browser. It is not sent to any server, the owner's included, so the owner does not receive, see or keep it. The page runs under a security policy (Content-Security-Policy) that stops the browser from connecting to any server once the page has loaded.

Closing or reloading the tab erases what you wrote from the browser's memory.

The Word file you download

The Word file you download carries a copy of your CV's data, so you can upload it later and keep editing. It only includes what your CV shows: hidden sections are not saved. The file stays on your device, under your control. If you send it to someone, they also receive that copy; if you would rather not share it, send the PDF.

Technical connection data

As with any website, when you visit the site your browser connects to the hosting server, Vercel Inc., which receives and may log for a time technical data: IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and operating system.

  • Purpose: delivering the page and protecting it against abuse and attacks.
  • Legal basis: the legitimate interest in running a site that works and is secure (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
  • Processor: Vercel Inc. processes this data on the owner's behalf as the hosting provider.
  • International transfers: Vercel may process this data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.
  • Retention: the period Vercel sets for its logs. The owner does not export them or combine them with other data.

No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used, and no profiles are built.

Cookies and storage

This site uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. More detail in the cookie policy.
